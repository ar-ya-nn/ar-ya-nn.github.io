Projects

Predictive Digital-Twin Traffic Engineering for SDN

In progress

Computer Networks course project, VIT Vellore

A digital twin of a software-defined network that forecasts traffic and lets the controller act before congestion happens, instead of reacting after links are already overloaded.

  • Emulating the network in Mininet, controlled through a Ryu/ONOS SDN controller.
  • Modelling the topology and candidate paths in Python with NetworkX.
  • Forecasting link load with an LSTM/TCN model to drive predictive traffic engineering.
  • Mininet
  • Ryu / ONOS
  • Python
  • NetworkX
  • LSTM / TCN
